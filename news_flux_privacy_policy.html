Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to NewsFlux ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. NewsFlux is designed as an anonymous, friction-free news aggregator. You can read, swipe through, and bookmark news without creating an account or logging in.

2. Data Collection and Usage

We do not require account creation, nor do we collect or store your name, email address, phone number, or personal contacts on our servers.

However, to provide user customization and serve advertisements, certain non-personally identifiable information is processed:

  • Local Preference Data: Your selected news topics and bookmarked articles are saved strictly on your local device storage. This data does not leave your device.
  • Third-Party Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob automatically collects and processes certain diagnostic and device data to display relevant ads.

3. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

NewsFlux integrates third-party SDKs that may collect information used to identify your device. Third-party vendor information collection includes:

  • Advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • IP Address (used for coarse location estimation)
  • Device diagnostic, crash, and app performance logs

For detailed information on how Google uses and processes your data, please review:

Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising

4. User Rights and Controls

You have total control over your local data and personalized experience:

  • Managing Bookmarks: You can add or delete saved news articles from the Bookmarks tab at any time.
  • Ad Personalization: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising identifiers at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Children's Privacy

NewsFlux does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can immediately address the situation.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
